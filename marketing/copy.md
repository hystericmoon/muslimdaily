**LinkedIn**
> I've been building Adwam, a free Android app for daily dhikr and duas, and its core feature is a
> memorization flow in four steps:
>
> 1. Listen: phrase by phrase, slowed down if you like.
> 2. Build: one more phrase each round, repeated aloud.
> 3. Recall: hide 30, 60, then 100% of the words.
> 4. Check: tap what you missed and rate yourself honestly; the next review is scheduled for you.
>
> It's fully offline (no internet permission at all), has no ads, analytics or trackers, and it's
> open source. Light and dark themes, WCAG AA contrast.
>
> https://hystericmoon.github.io/muslimdaily/
>
> #Android #OpenSource #Accessibility #ProductDesign
