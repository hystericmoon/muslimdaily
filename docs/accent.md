## Where it fits

Today the 60 is the surface (parchment `#FBF8F1`, soft dark `#121412`, tinted per palette), the 30 is
the palette colour, and the 10 is gold, used only as a fill for the active phrase and completed counts
(it cannot carry text on a light page). The built-in tertiary is a quiet blue-grey. There was no strong
accent for "this is happening now". The new accent is that colour.

## How it was chosen

- Each palette keeps its seed as the 30. The accent hue sits at least 50 degrees from the palette's own
  colour (OKLCH), at least 30 degrees from gold (90) and from the error red (28), and the eight
  accents are spread around the wheel (30 degrees or more apart) so no two palettes share one.
  A single rule per palette first gave four near-identical blues; the hues below were assigned instead,
  with Teal (the default) fixed to iris violet.
- Tone is then set so the accent reaches 4.6:1 on the surface and about 4.6:1 on the highest surface
  container in each mode; text on the accent container reaches 7:1. Chroma 0.13 for the accent,
  0.045 to 0.06 for the container.
- Gold stays as it is. Error and prohibited times stay red with an icon and words.

Regenerate or check: `python3 tools/accent_palette.py` (table) or `--json`. It reads the palette
tokens in `docs/design-tokens.md`, so a new palette needs a hue added to `HUES` there.

## Where the accent may be used (about a tenth of a screen at most)

Today: "in 7 h 1 min" on the Next card, a thin progress line under it (new; drop it if the owner
prefers colours only), the "Now" chip on the current prayer, the countdown on the next prayer row.
Later: the island countdown and big card, the selected day in the Hijri and fasting calendars, a
"New" chip and the What's New dot, the Qibla "Aligned" marker.

Never for body or Arabic text, errors or prohibited times, the active-phrase highlight or counts
(gold), or more than about a tenth of a screen.
