# Adwam brand files

Adwam (أدوم) means *the most constant*, after the hadith that the deeds most beloved to Allah are
the most constant, even if few. Wherever that hadith's text, translation or reference is shown,
it comes from the sourced content, like every other narration in the app.

The mark is **Wasl** (joining): one line runs along the baseline, loops back once and keeps going,
then a small gold dot: the day coming round again, the line carrying on, the small deed kept.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `adwam-lockup.svg` | Default lockup (mark, **adwam**, **أدوم**) on parchment `#FBF8F1` or white. |
| `adwam-lockup-dark.svg` | On `#121412` and other dark grounds. |
| `adwam-lockup-latin.svg` | Without the Arabic name, for small sizes (under 120px wide use the mark). |
| `adwam-mark.svg` | The mark alone, teal `#1F5F5B` and gold `#C9A227`. Generated by `tools/brand/build_mark.py`. |
| `adwam-mark-reversed.svg` | Parchment line and gold dot, for teal grounds. |
| `adwam-mark-mono.svg` | One ink. |
| `adwam-app-icon.svg`, `adwam-app-icon-512.png` | Launcher art and the Play Store icon (512 × 512, opaque). |

Rules:

- The line is exactly as thick as the dot is wide; the dot sits half a line-width past the line's
  end; the loop leans forward about 7°; the baseline stays level and unbroken under the loop.
- The line is teal on light grounds, `#81D5CE` on dark, parchment on teal. The dot is always gold,
  except in the one-ink version. Gold never carries text (2.3:1 on parchment).
- The mark works from 16px; the full lockup from 120px wide. Clear space: the height of the loop's
  counter on every side.
- The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans 700 at -3% tracking and the Arabic is Noto Naskh Arabic 700,
  both outlined in the files. Never retype them.
- Launcher art keeps the mark 56 of 108 units wide, centred, so it fits the safe zone under any mask.

To change the mark, edit `tools/brand/build_mark.py` (needs `pip install shapely`) and run it; it
rewrites the launcher, themed, notification and Wear drawables and `adwam-mark.svg`.
