# Colour tokens

Generated by `tools/color/generate_scheme.mjs`. Seed `#1F5F5B`, tonal spot. Surfaces
overridden to `#FBF8F1` (light) and `#121412` (dark); container steps are
taken from a low-chroma palette built on each surface's own hue so cards stay warm on
parchment and neutral on the dark surface.

Gold `#C9A227` is used only as a fill or non-text indicator:

| Role | Light | Dark | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `highlightFill` | #ffe08e | #584400 | Background of the phrase being recited |
| `onHighlightFill` | #241a00 | #ffe08e | Arabic text sitting on that fill |
| `highlightIndicator` | #006a65 (primary) | #c9a227 (gold) | Underline under the active phrase |
| `completeFill` | #c9a227 | #c9a227 | Count-complete disc |
| `onCompleteFill` | #241a00 | #241a00 | Check glyph inside the disc |

In light mode the underline uses primary teal rather than gold because raw gold on parchment
is below 3:1. The active phrase is therefore marked by fill + underline + TalkBack state, never
by colour alone.
